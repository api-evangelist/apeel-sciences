Apeel (Apeel Sciences) is a food technology company founded in 2012 and headquartered in Goleta,
California. It produces a plant-derived, edible coating — made from lipids and glycerolipids found in
the peels, seeds and pulp of produce — applied to fresh fruit and vegetables at packing houses to slow
water loss and oxidation, extending shelf life and reducing food waste across the supply chain. In 2021
Apeel acquired ImpactVision, adding hyperspectral imaging to its supplier installations to grade
interior quality, ripeness and freshness.
